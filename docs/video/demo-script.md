# 30-second demo video: script

AI-assisted: first draft written by Claude (Anthropic), reviewed by the team.

Rule: video of at most 30 seconds, public link. No narration, so nothing has to be translated: the English
captions come from `demo.srt` (upload it as subtitles, or burn them in with a video editor).
No people in the video. Record the **published site** (Vercel address) in a window about 1400 x 850,
Educational mode first. Use the real values on screen; do not edit numbers.

| Time | On screen | Caption (same as `demo.srt`) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:04 | Map of the south pole with the nine region markers and the region list | The lunar south pole: nine Artemis III candidate regions. |
| 0:04 to 0:10 | Click **3D Moon** (top bar). Drag once to rotate, scroll once to zoom toward the orange markers | Switch to the 3D Moon and explore where they sit. |
| 0:10 to 0:15 | Click **Malapert Massif** in the list: the Moon turns to it and the marker turns white | Pick a site: how much sunlight, how much Earth in view? |
| 0:15 to 0:21 | Panel: cards "Sunlight 37.6 %" and "Earth in view 45.5 %", then scroll to the margin chart | Malapert Massif: 38% of the time in sunlight, 46% with the Earth in view. |
| 0:21 to 0:26 | Click **Expert**: parameters and the Horizons query links (the view returns to the map) | Expert mode: same numbers, with parameters and the exact JPL Horizons query. |
| 0:26 to 0:30 | Map with all regions visible; footer with the NASA notice in view | Built with NASA LOLA and JPL Horizons data. Not an official NASA product. |

## Recording on Zorin OS (GNOME)

1. Press **Ctrl + Shift + Alt + R** to start the screen recorder (a red dot appears) and the same keys to stop.
   GNOME stops it by itself at 30 s by default. The file goes to the `Videos` folder (WebM).
2. Rehearse once without recording. Move the mouse slowly; the cursor is the only "narrator".
3. If a take is longer than 30 s, cut it (Shotcut or Kdenlive) before uploading. The limit is strict.

## Publishing

- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** (or Public), never Private. Add `demo.srt` as English subtitles.
- Title: "Lunar Landing Site Navigator - NASA Space Apps 2026 demo".
- Open the link in a private window to confirm it plays without login, then paste it in "Link to Project Demo".
